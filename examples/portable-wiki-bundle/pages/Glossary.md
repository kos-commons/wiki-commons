**Base revision.** The revision an editor loaded before making changes. ^base-revision

**Optimistic concurrency.** Letting everyone edit without locks and detecting
conflicts at save time by comparing base revisions. ^optimistic-concurrency

**Revision.** One saved state of a page, with time, author, and summary. ^revision
